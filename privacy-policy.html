Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Overview

Tree Service Jonesboro AR ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website at treeservicejonesboroar.com. This privacy policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We collect only what is necessary to respond to your service inquiries and improve our website.

Information We Collect

Contact Form Submissions

When you submit a contact or estimate request form, we collect the information you provide — typically your name, phone number, email address, service address, and a description of your tree care needs. This information is used solely to respond to your inquiry and schedule an on-site assessment if appropriate.

Phone Calls

When you call us directly at (870) 555-0190, standard phone call information may be retained for service scheduling and follow-up purposes. We do not record calls without disclosure.

Website Analytics

We may use analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) to understand how visitors interact with our website — including which pages are viewed, how long visitors stay, and how they arrive at the site. This data is aggregated and anonymous; it does not identify individual visitors. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies — small text files stored on your device — to remember preferences and support analytics. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect some website functionality.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to tree service inquiries and schedule on-site assessments
  • To provide written estimates and follow up on pending jobs
  • To improve website content and user experience through analytics
  • To communicate about scheduled work, changes, or follow-up items

We do not use your contact information for unsolicited marketing, and we do not add you to any email marketing lists without your explicit consent.

Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: Tools we use to operate our website (hosting, contact forms, analytics) may process data on our behalf under appropriate data protection agreements.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or in response to a valid legal process.

Data Retention

Contact form submissions and inquiry records are retained for as long as necessary to fulfill the service inquiry and any resulting work. Analytics data is retained according to the policies of the analytics provider (typically 26 months for Google Analytics).

Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you. To make such a request, contact us using the information below. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

External Links

Our website may contain links to external sites (such as Google Maps or social media profiles). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their privacy policies independently.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects when the policy was last revised. Continued use of our website after any update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
