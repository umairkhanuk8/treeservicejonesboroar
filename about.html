Who We Are

Local Tree Care Professionals Serving Northeast Arkansas

Tree Service Jonesboro AR is a locally operated tree care company based in Craighead County. We specialize in residential and commercial tree removal, trimming, pruning, stump grinding, storm-damage cleanup, emergency response, and land clearing — serving properties throughout the Northeast Arkansas region from the Jonesboro metro to rural communities across six surrounding counties.

We operate on a straightforward principle: visit the property, assess the actual conditions, explain clearly what we find, and provide a written estimate before any work is scheduled. Our team works with homeowners on established residential lots, landlords managing rental properties, commercial property managers with clearance and maintenance needs, and agricultural property owners with rural acreage clearing and removal requirements. The approach is the same regardless of property type: assess first, communicate clearly, work safely, and leave the site in appropriate condition when the job is done.

Our Approach

What Separates Our Tree Care from the Rest

Tree work varies significantly by property, tree condition, access, and surrounding infrastructure. We account for all of that — not just the tree size.

📋

On-Site Assessment First

Every estimate begins with a property visit. We evaluate the tree's structure, visible defects, root zone, canopy balance, access routes, and surrounding site conditions before putting numbers on paper. Phone estimates for tree work are rarely accurate — site conditions matter too much.

📝

Written, Site-Specific Estimates

We provide estimates in writing after the site visit so you know exactly what work is included, how debris will be handled, and what the job scope covers before any crew arrives. No vague verbal quotes, no scope creep on the day of the job.

✅

Honest Recommendations

We don't recommend removal when pruning is the appropriate response, and we don't inflate the scope to increase billing. When a tree can be safely managed through targeted trimming or deadwood removal, we say so. When removal is genuinely the right call, we explain why based on what we actually found.

🌿

Proper Pruning Practices

We make pruning cuts at the branch collar, not stubs, and we don't perform topping — a practice that creates large wounds, accelerates decay, and produces structurally weak regrowth regardless of species. Proper cuts allow the tree to close wounds over time and maintain structural integrity.

🛡️

Safe, Planned Work Sequences

Before any cutting begins, work zones are established, rigging is planned for restricted fall zones, and the crew knows the sequence. We don't work near energized utility lines, and storm-damage jobs are approached with extra caution because suspended loads and tension in damaged wood create unpredictable forces.

🏡

Full Site Cleanup Included

Brush is chipped and removed. Log sections are handled per your preference. The site is raked clean and reviewed before we leave. Stump grinding is done at the agreed depth when included in scope. The area is left in a condition appropriate for its intended use.

How We Work

Our Tree Care Process, Step by Step

From your first call to final site cleanup — here is exactly what working with our team looks like.

You Contact Us

Call or submit an online request. Describe the general situation — we'll ask a few questions to understand the property type and the nature of the tree concern so we can schedule the site visit efficiently.

On-Site Assessment

We visit the property and evaluate the tree's structural condition, canopy balance, root zone, visible defects, and surrounding site. Access routes, nearby structures, utility proximity, and equipment positioning are all factored in.

Written Estimate

Based on what we found, we prepare a written estimate covering the work scope, removal or pruning method, debris handling plan, and stump grinding if applicable. You review and approve before any work is scheduled.

Scheduled Work Day

The crew arrives with the appropriate equipment for the site conditions. Work zones are established, rigging is set up where needed, and the job proceeds in a planned sequence that protects surrounding structures, landscaping, and infrastructure.

Cleanup & Final Review

All brush is chipped and removed. Log sections are handled per your preference. The site is raked and reviewed against the estimate scope. You walk the property with us before we leave to confirm everything was completed as agreed.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About Our Company

Where is Tree Service Jonesboro AR based?
▼

We are based in Jonesboro, Arkansas, and serve residential, commercial, and rural properties throughout Craighead County and surrounding counties in Northeast Arkansas — including Greene, Poinsett, Lawrence, Randolph, and Mississippi counties. Our coverage area extends from communities near Jonesboro outward to Paragould, Trumann, Harrisburg, Walnut Ridge, Pocahontas, Manila, and Leachville.

Do you provide free estimates?
▼

Yes. We provide free written estimates after an on-site assessment of your property. We do not price jobs over the phone because tree condition, site access, and surrounding infrastructure all affect the scope and price of the work — and those factors can only be evaluated accurately in person. The estimate is provided in writing before any work is scheduled.

Are you licensed and insured?
▼

Yes. We carry general liability insurance on all tree care work. Before any job begins, we assess the worksite for hazards, establish work-zone boundaries, and follow established safety protocols to protect both property and personnel. Proof of insurance is available upon request before work is scheduled.

Do you work on rural and agricultural properties?
▼

Yes. Rural acreage, farm properties, fence-line clearing, agricultural border clearing, and properties with soft-ground or long-distance access conditions are a significant part of our workload across Northeast Arkansas. We assess access conditions, ground conditions, and surrounding farm infrastructure on every rural property before providing any estimate — rural property situations require different planning than suburban residential jobs.

Do you handle emergency and storm-damage tree work?
▼

Yes. We respond to storm-damaged and hazardous tree situations throughout our Northeast Arkansas service area. Storm-damage response begins with an on-site assessment before any cutting begins — this is especially important when a tree is under load against a structure or when damaged wood is under tension, as unplanned cutting can cause unpredictable and dangerous movement. If overhead utility lines are involved, contact your utility provider before approaching the area.

Do you perform tree topping?
▼

No. We do not perform topping — a practice that involves cutting main stems and large branches back to stubs without regard for proper pruning cuts. Topping creates large, exposed wounds that are slow to close, accelerates decay in the trunk and remaining branches, and produces structurally weak, fast-growing regrowth that tends to fail more readily than the original branch structure. When a tree's height or canopy spread is a concern, selective crown reduction using proper pruning cuts is the appropriate approach — and even then, it has limits that we discuss during the assessment.
