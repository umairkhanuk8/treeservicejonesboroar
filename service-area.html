County Coverage

Counties in Our Service Area

Our Northeast Arkansas tree care team serves properties across multiple counties. Each county presents its own terrain, property types, and tree-care considerations.

🌿

Craighead County

Primary service county. Covers Jonesboro, Brookland, Bono, Bay, Lake City, Monette, and surrounding rural and suburban communities along US-49, US-63, and Highway 18 corridors.

🌿

Greene County

Northeast of Craighead County. Serves Paragould and surrounding Greene County communities along US-49 and US-412. Mix of established residential neighborhoods and rural agricultural land.

🌿

Poinsett County

South of Craighead County along US-49. Serves Trumann, Harrisburg, and surrounding Poinsett County communities. Delta agricultural character with drainage and soil moisture considerations.

🌿

Lawrence County

Northwest along US-63. Serves Walnut Ridge and surrounding Lawrence County communities. More varied terrain than eastern Delta areas, including rolling landscape and forested acreage.

🌿

Randolph County

Northern reach along US-67. Serves Pocahontas and Randolph County. Distinctive terrain with Black River riparian areas, hillier landscape, and more heavily forested rural acreage.

🌿

Mississippi County

East of Craighead County into the Arkansas Delta. Serves Manila, Leachville, and surrounding Mississippi County communities. Open agricultural Delta landscape with drainage infrastructure and large farm properties.

Don't See Your Community Listed?

We serve communities throughout Northeast Arkansas beyond those listed here. Contact us to discuss coverage for your property location—rural acreage, small communities, and properties outside our listed service areas can often be accommodated.
