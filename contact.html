Our Process

What to Expect After You Contact Us

From your first call or form submission through completed work — here is exactly how the process works.

  • 1

    We Respond and Schedule the Site Visit

    We'll call or email within one business day to get a few details about your property and tree situation, then schedule a time to come out for the assessment. For emergency situations involving fallen or hazardous trees, call us directly at (870) 555-0190 — don't wait for a form response.

  • 2

    On-Site Assessment of Your Property and Tree

    We visit the property, walk the site, and evaluate the tree's condition, structure, root zone, available access, fall zone, and surrounding infrastructure. This step is not skippable — tree work pricing depends on factors that can only be evaluated in person.

  • 3

    Written Estimate With Full Scope

    Based on what we found during the site visit, we provide a written estimate covering the work method, debris handling plan, equipment to be used, and whether stump grinding is included. You review it and approve before we schedule the job.

  • 4

    Scheduled Work, Full Cleanup, and Site Review

    The crew arrives at the agreed time with the appropriate equipment. Work proceeds in a planned sequence that protects your property. After the job, the site is cleaned and you walk it with us to confirm everything was completed as agreed. For commercial properties, documentation of work completed is provided.

Common Questions

Common Questions About Reaching Our Team

How long does it take to get an estimate after I contact you?
▼

We typically schedule on-site estimates within 1–3 business days of initial contact, depending on current workload. Emergency situations involving active hazards — a tree on a structure, hanging wood over an occupied area, or a blocked driveway — are prioritized. Call (870) 555-0190 directly for the fastest response on urgent situations.

Do you give estimates over the phone or by email?
▼

No. We provide written estimates only after an on-site assessment of the tree and property. Tree work pricing depends on factors that cannot be evaluated remotely: the tree's structural condition, available access for equipment, the fall zone, rigging requirements, and debris volume. Phone or email quotes for tree work are almost never accurate — we won't give you one, and you shouldn't trust one from anyone who does.

What information should I have ready when I call?
▼

You don't need to have everything figured out before you call. A general description of the situation is enough to get started: the property address, a rough description of the tree or the concern (dead tree, hanging limb, storm damage, lot clearing, etc.), and any notes on access or urgency. We'll ask additional questions when we schedule the site visit. If it's an emergency, just call and describe what's happening.

Do you handle emergencies outside of normal business hours?
▼

Yes, on a case-by-case basis for genuine hazards. If a tree has fallen onto a structure, is actively threatening an occupied area, or is blocking emergency access, call us directly at (870) 555-0190. We assess true emergency situations outside normal hours based on the nature and severity of the hazard. For non-urgent after-hours requests, submit a form or leave a voicemail and we'll respond the next business day.

Do you serve my community? I'm not in Jonesboro city limits.
▼

Our service area extends well beyond Jonesboro city limits into surrounding communities across Northeast Arkansas — including Brookland, Bono, Bay, Lake City, Monette, Paragould, Trumann, Harrisburg, Walnut Ridge, Pocahontas, Manila, Leachville, and rural properties in Craighead, Greene, Poinsett, Lawrence, Randolph, and Mississippi counties. If you're outside these areas, contact us — rural properties and communities outside our listed coverage can often be accommodated.
